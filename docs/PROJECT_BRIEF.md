# Project brief

Normalized from `resources/Tattoo website instructions.pdf`, authored by Amy Jane van den Bergh and dated 28 July 2026. The PDF remains the source of truth.

## Business and positioning

- Business: **seriously ridiculous tattoos.**
- Tagline: **get inky with AJ**
- Artist: Amy Jane van den Bergh.
- Location: Netherlands; Amy describes the business as a mobile studio.
- Contact: `inky.amyjane@gmail.com`, WhatsApp `0617805417`, Instagram `@inky.amyjane`.
- Bookings and consultations are by appointment.

Amy is a South African multidisciplinary artist, tattooist, and screenprinter with a 20-year career in printmaking and painting. Her differentiator is a collaborative custom-design process grounded in clients' stories, symbolism, and aesthetics, rather than reproducing Pinterest images.

### Intended audiences

- Expats documenting meaningful parts of life abroad.
- Bird lovers and artists.
- Open-minded people seeking custom work.
- Brand voice note from Amy: "No Karens."

### Tattoo range

- Botanicals and birds.
- Fine line.
- Realism.
- Colour.
- Geometrical designs.
- Broad versatility rooted in a multidisciplinary art practice.

## Brand direction

### Personality

- Warm and friendly.
- Zero-fuss, clear, and direct.
- Humorous and slightly whimsical.
- Bold and colourful.
- Professional and trustworthy.

### Visual language

- Generous off-white negative space.
- Pink, orange, yellow, and green.
- Minimalist pop-art energy.
- Typography somewhere between monospace and handwriting, while remaining readable.
- Succinct copy.
- Clear, persistent top navigation and a one-page-flow feeling.
- Avoid dark/heavy styling, fussy navigation, busy pages, and excess imagery or text.

The supplied circular-gallery examples are `resources/IMG_7307.png` and `resources/IMG_7308.png`. They show evenly spaced circular crops on white, with both colour and desaturated treatments. Treat them as compositional references, not final assets.

## Information architecture

Amy asks for the feel of a one-page flow while naming five content areas:

1. About, including prices.
2. Portfolio: tattoos, available flash, and client reviews.
3. FAQs, aftercare, and consent form.
4. Contact/request a quote.
5. Shop for T-shirts and screenprints.

The eventual architecture may use anchored sections plus dedicated legal/form or commerce routes, but that is not decided.

The first client-review deployment will be a static GitHub Pages project site. It is a preview target, not the final commercial/shop host.

## Core behavior

- Mobile-first and fast loading.
- Easy for Amy to update.
- Booking/contact form.
- Image gallery.
- Cookie handling appropriate to the actual technologies used.
- Future online shop.
- Clear call to action throughout.

### Portfolio

- Exactly 25 final tattoo works are desired.
- Separate rows/sections for tattoo photos, available flash, and reviews.
- Circular, desaturated thumbnails with full-colour enlarged views on activation.
- A single horizontal, swipeable line per section; the brief describes five visible images per line.
- Swiping cannot be the only control. Keyboard and visible previous/next controls are required.

### Booking form

Requested fields:

- Customer name.
- Phone number.
- Email address.
- Short tattoo-request description.

### FAQs and consent

FAQ topics requested:

1. Minimum age.
2. How to book.
3. Cost.
4. Pain.
5. Healing time.
6. Aftercare.
7. Payment.
8. Design and creation process.
9. The Seriously Ridiculous tattoo experience.
10. Studio location.

Amy wants compact, expandable text controls rather than a wall of copy. She asks to model the experience on Sorry Mama Tattoos, but the content and consent fields must be based on current official Dutch requirements.

The signed consent form should be delivered to Amy and the customer. Delivery, secure storage, retention, access, and deletion behavior remain undecided.

### Shop

- Online shop for T-shirts and screenprints.
- Amy must be able to manage it herself.
- Customers wearing one of Amy's T-shirts to an appointment receive 10% off their tattoo.
- Status: coming soon; stock is not yet available.
